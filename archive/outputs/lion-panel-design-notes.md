## Modeling direction

Square shallow panel, broad enclosed regions, four simple background panes, substantial black border and seams. Translucency has not been verified with these spools; test thin swatches before choosing panel thickness. The artwork can also be used as an opaque decorative panel.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Create a clean flat front-view design concept for a 3D-printable stained-glass-style PLA panel featuring Apple's lion face emoji, adapted into simple closed color regions. Square panel with slightly rounded corners and a substantial continuous black outer frame. Centered large friendly lion head, symmetrical Apple emoji proportions: full rounded scalloped cocoa-brown mane, small round ears, golden-yellow face, large simple black oval eyes, white paired muzzle lobes, black rounded triangular nose and short friendly mouth. Preserve recognizable Apple lion character, simplifying away gradients, fur strands, whisker dots and tiny highlights.
Use ONLY five flat solid colors sampled from user's available Bambu PLA Basic library: Black #000000, Cocoa Brown #6F5034, Sunflower Yellow #FEC600, Jade White #FFFFFF, Cyan #0086D6. Mane one brown color divided into only 6-8 broad simple sections. Face one yellow color, white muzzle. Cyan background divided into only four large panes by black seams extending from mane to frame. Bold consistent black boundaries, rounded junctions, large contiguous regions, no thin slivers. All shapes enclosed. A clean 2D fabrication-oriented artwork, no perspective, no gradients, no glass texture, no dramatic lighting, no physical mockup, no text, no legend, no extra ornaments. Panel occupies almost entire square image with a small white outside margin. Aim for an approachable emoji stained glass design that could be extruded into a shallow relief; not a realistic lion or intricate traditional stained glass.
