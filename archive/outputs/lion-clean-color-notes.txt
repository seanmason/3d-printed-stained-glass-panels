PHYSICAL CHECK BEFORE TEST PRINT
Remove black residue from nozzle tips / silicone socks and the wiping pad.
Inspect the idle-nozzle flow blocker for residue, damage, or a visible sealing gap.
Let the nozzles cool to room temperature before inspecting/adjusting the blocker.
Dry suspect filament using Bambu's instructions for that exact material/spool.
These physical checks have not been performed remotely.

Reference: Bambu Lab H2D 3D Printing User Manual, sections 9.7, 11.8.4, 11.8.5
https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/4668d0ca43994ff3bff4b37f1a65c2e7.pdf

If tint remains within the deposited light filament, investigate purge carryover.
If clean-color hairs/streaks remain on top, prioritize nozzle/wiper/blocker and drying.
Assess the printed result before making further temperature/retraction changes.
