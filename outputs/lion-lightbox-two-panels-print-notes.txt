LION LIGHT-BOX INSERTS - H2D

Plate 1: Winking lion (left), original non-winking lion v5 (right).
Plate 2: Original light-box housing, unchanged.
Plate 3: Original light guide, unchanged.

Dimensions, each insert: 108 x 144 mm. Black perimeter and dividers: 2.5 mm tall.
Colored sections: 0.8 mm thick, flat underside. Lion proportions preserved.
Mounting outline and maximum rim thickness match the original lithophane exactly.
Actual printed fit is not yet physically tested.

PLA Basic project color numbers:
1 Jade White - muzzle and highlights; original light guide
2 Cyan - background
3 Sunflower Yellow - face and outer ears
4 Pumpkin Orange - upper mane
5 Charcoal Black - original housing only
6 Cocoa Brown - lower mane and inner ears
7 Black - insert perimeter, dividers, eyes, nose and mouth

H2D: 0.4 mm standard nozzles, one AMS on each nozzle.
Insert object overrides: 0.1 mm layers, 100% infill, no supports, no brim.
The first layer remains 0.2 mm. Total insert height is 2.5 mm (24 layers).
Keep both panels as multipart objects so every region retains its assigned color.
The purge tower on plate 1 was moved to X=40, Y=245 for bed clearance.
Thin PLA color panes may transmit light, with brightness depending on filament.

Before sending a print, use filament names/numbers to map the six insert colors to
physical AMS slots. Project color numbers are not physical AMS slot numbers.
